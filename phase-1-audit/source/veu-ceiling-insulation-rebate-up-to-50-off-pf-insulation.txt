Victorian Ceiling Insulation Rebate & VEU Discount
The Victorian Energy Upgrades program is introducing a new ceiling insulation
  activity designed to help Victorian households improve energy efficiency,
  comfort and thermal performance.
From
1 October 2026
, the activity expands to eligible Victorian
  homes with uninsulated or under-insulated ceilings.
According to the Victorian Government, the VEU discount is expected to reduce
  the average eligible ceiling insulation installation cost by approximately
30–50%
.
Important:
This is not a fixed cash payment to homeowners. The VEU financial benefit is
applied through the eligible installation process and depends on factors such
as property size, location, certificate value, installation costs and the
applicable VEU requirements.

VEU Ceiling Insulation Safety Requirements
Installing insulation in a roof space requires careful attention to
  electrical, fire, access and working-at-heights hazards.
⚡ Electrical Safety
A Pre-Installation Electrical Safety Assessment must be completed by
      the appropriate licensed electrician before eligible work proceeds.
🦺 Installer Safety
The installation must be assessed for workplace hazards and appropriate
      safe work controls before work begins.
🔥 Product Compliance
Products used for the VEU activity must meet the applicable product,
      thermal and fire-safety requirements.
📋 Installation Standards
Insulation installation must follow the applicable VEU requirements,
      manufacturer requirements and relevant Australian Standards.

Frequently Asked Questions About the VEU Insulation Rebate
When does the Victorian ceiling insulation rebate start?
The VEU ceiling insulation activity expands to all eligible Victorian homes
    with uninsulated or under-insulated ceilings from
1 October 2026
.
How much is the ceiling insulation rebate in Victoria?
The Victorian Government expects the VEU discount to reduce average eligible
    installation costs by approximately 30–50%. Government examples indicate
    incentives of up to approximately $1,482 for an average 150 m² metropolitan
    Melbourne home and up to approximately $2,104 for an average 150 m² regional
    Victorian home. Actual benefits vary.
Who qualifies for the Victorian insulation rebate?
The activity is intended for eligible Victorian homes with no ceiling
    insulation or existing ceiling insulation below R2.0, subject to the full
    VEU requirements and safety checks.
Is the VEU insulation rebate paid in cash?
No. The VEU benefit is applied through the eligible upgrade process.
    Customers do not receive the VEEC value as a separate cash payment.
What is the minimum customer contribution?
The Victorian Government states that the minimum customer contribution
    for the VEU ceiling insulation activity is $200 including GST.
Do I need an electrical safety check?
Yes. A Pre-Installation Electrical Safety Assessment is required before
    eligible ceiling insulation work under the applicable VEU requirements.
    The assessment identifies electrical hazards and any rectification needed.
Can I remove my old insulation to qualify?
Existing insulation should not be removed simply to create eligibility.
    If old insulation genuinely needs to be removed because it is damaged,
    contaminated or unsuitable, PF Insulation can assess and quote the
    removal separately.
Can I choose R6 or R7 insulation?
Higher R-value options may be available depending on the product,
    roof-space conditions and applicable installation requirements.
Is PF Insulation the Victorian Government?
No. PF Insulation is an independent insulation contractor. We work with
    a VEU Accredited Provider for applicable VEU ceiling insulation activities.
